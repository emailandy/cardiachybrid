## Task
S02 — S3 defibrillator audio
Implement Sensor S3 defibrillator acoustic charge and shock detection in `sensors/s3_defib.py`, calibration profiles in `sensors/calibration/defib_profiles.yaml`, and unit tests in `tests/test_s3.py`.
Tracks rising charge tones (primary) and acoustic discharge transient spikes (corroborating). Uncharged or un-discharged tones classify as `defib_charge_aborted`.

Sensors are DETERMINISTIC. Every threshold is CALIBRATED against ground truth, never chosen because it looked right. Thresholds live in `sensors/calibration/defib_profiles.yaml`, keyed by device model, never hardcoded.
